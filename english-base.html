兒童英文拼音及基礎課程

📚 兒童英文拼音及基礎課程 (K3至小一，由基礎起步，循序漸進銜接至小六)

結合英文拼音與基礎文法，採用遊戲式教學，融入不同教學元素，讓學習變得生動有趣，讓小朋友同時掌握拼音及基礎文法概念。課程同時擴充詞彙量及閱讀理解能力，為升小一英文作準備，建立對英文的信心。課堂輔以適量練習，鞏固所學。

  • 拼音：涵蓋字母短音、常見組合音、長音、混合音等，可銜接IPA國際音標。
  • 文法：涵蓋基礎文法概念，包括詞彙分類、基本句子結構等。
  • 英文基礎：涵蓋擴充詞彙量、基礎文法、閱讀理解、見字識讀、聽音識串。

小一英文 (P1 English)

  • 自然拼讀與發音 (Phonics & Pronunciation)：Letter Sounds（字母發音）、Short Vowels（短元音）、Blends（輔音連讀）
  • 語法知識 (Grammar)：Subject Pronouns & Verb "to be"、Singular / Plural Nouns、Articles (a/an/the)、Demonstrative Pronouns、Prepositions of Location、Imperatives、Punctuation、Question Words
  • 閱讀與寫作 (Reading & Writing)：Big Book Reading & Sight Words、Sentence Writing / Guided Writing

小二英文 (P2 English)

  • 語法與詞彙 (Grammar & Vocabulary)：Simple Present Tense、Present Continuous Tense、Possessive Adjectives、Prepositions of Location & Method、Quantifiers、Conjunctions、Adverbs & Verb "to be"、Proper Nouns & Common Nouns

小三英文 (P3 English)

  • 語法知識 (Grammar)：Simple Past Tense、Future Tense、Comparatives & Superlatives、Modal Verbs、Adverbs of Frequency & Manner、Prepositions of Time、Countable & Uncountable Nouns、Conjunctions、Wh-Questions
  • 寫作與閱讀 (Writing & Reading)：Guided Writing / Short Writing

小四英文 (P4 English)

  • 語法知識 (Grammar)：Present Perfect Tense、Past Continuous Tense、Relative Clauses、Conditional Sentences - Type 1、Passive Voice (Introductory)、Reported Speech (Introductory)、Phrasal Verbs
  • 閱讀與寫作 (Reading & Writing)：Reading Comprehension (Longer Passages)、Writing (Essays, Diaries, Letters/Emails)

小五英文 (P5 English)

  • 語法知識 (Grammar)：All Tenses Review & Application、Relative Clauses (Advanced)、Conditional Sentences (Type 1 & 2)、Passive Voice、Reported Speech、Connectors & Discourse Markers、Modal Verbs、Gerunds
  • 詞彙建構 (Vocabulary Building)：Phrasal Verbs & Idioms、Prefixes & Suffixes、Academic & Theme Words
  • 寫作 (Text Types & Writing)：Short Essays, Letters, Emails, Reports, Travelogues, Persuasive Writing

小六英文 (P6 English)

  • 語法知識 (Grammar)：All Tenses (Comprehensive Application)、Advanced Relative Clauses、Conditional Sentences (Type 1-3)、Passive Voice across All Tenses、Reported Speech (Advanced)、Phrasal Verbs & Idioms
  • 文體與寫作 (Text Types & Writing)：Narrative, Descriptive, Explanatory, Persuasive, Practical Texts
