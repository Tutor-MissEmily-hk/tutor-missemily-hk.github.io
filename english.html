課程簡介

結合英文拼音與基礎文法，採用遊戲式教學，擴充詞彙量及閱讀理解能力，為升小一英文作準備，並循序漸進銜接至小六大綱。

P1 - P6 英文科核心大綱

P1 English：

• Phonics: Short Vowels, Blends, Sight Words.

• Grammar: Subject Pronouns, Verb "to be", Singular/Plural Nouns, Prepositions.

P2 English：

• Grammar: Simple Present Tense, Present Continuous Tense, Adjectives, Conjunctions.

P3 English：

• Grammar: Simple Past Tense, Future Tense, Comparatives/Superlatives, Modal Verbs.

P4 English：

• Grammar: Present Perfect Tense, Past Continuous Tense, Conditionals, Relative Clauses.

P5 - P6 English：

• Grammar & Writing: All Tenses Review, Passive Voice, Reported Speech, Text Types Writing.
