P1 - P6 英國語文科課程大綱

小一英文 (P1 English)
  • Phonics & Pronunciation: Letter Sounds, Short Vowels, Blends (br, cl).
  • Grammar: Subject Pronouns & Verb "to be", Singular / Plural Nouns (-s), Articles (a/an/the), Demonstrative Pronouns, Prepositions of Location, Imperatives, Punctuation (Capital letters), Question Words.
  • Reading & Writing: Big Book Reading & Sight Words, Sentence Writing / Guided Writing.
小二英文 (P2 English)
  • Grammar & Vocabulary: Simple Present Tense, Present Continuous Tense, Possessive Adjectives, Prepositions of Location & Method, Quantifiers, Conjunctions, Adverbs & Verb "to be", Proper Nouns & Common Nouns.
小三英文 (P3 English)
  • Grammar: Simple Past Tense, Future Tense, Comparatives & Superlatives, Modal Verbs, Adverbs of Frequency & Manner, Prepositions of Time, Countable & Uncountable Nouns, Conjunctions, Wh-Questions.
  • Writing & Reading: Guided Writing / Short Writing.
小四英文 (P4 English)
  • Grammar: Present Perfect Tense, Past Continuous Tense, Relative Clauses, Conditional Sentences - Type 1 (If...), Passive Voice - Introductory, Reported Speech - Introductory, Phrasal Verbs.
  • Reading & Writing: Reading Comprehension - Longer Passages, Writing (Essays, Diaries, Letters/Emails).
小五英文 (P5 English)
  • Grammar: All Tenses Review & Application, Relative Clauses - Advanced, Conditional Sentences - Type 1 & 2, Passive Voice, Reported Speech, Connectors & Discourse Markers, Modal Verbs, Gerunds.
  • Vocabulary Building: Phrasal Verbs & Idioms, Prefixes & Suffixes, Academic & Theme Words.
  • Text Types & Writing: Short Essays, Letters, Emails, Reports, Travelogues, Persuasive Writing.
小六英文 (P6 English)
  • Grammar: All Tenses - Comprehensive Application, Advanced Relative Clauses, Conditional Sentences - Type 1-3, Passive Voice across All Tenses, Reported Speech - Advanced, Phrasal Verbs & Idioms.
  • Text Types & Writing: Narrative, Descriptive, Explanatory, Persuasive, Practical Texts.
